# Monetization Principles

## Do
- Game passes: e.g., extra island space, auto-collect, VIP area.
- Developer products: boosts, convenience.
- Cosmetics over raw power where possible.
- Clear prices, clear value.
- Premium Payouts (earned from Premium members' playtime).

## Don't
- Fake countdown pressure on purchases.
- Hidden or confusing pricing.
- Paid gambling aimed at minors. If any paid random items exist, follow [[Roblox Policies]].
  - **Loot Crates aren't counted as gambling (your call, 2026-11-10):** "its the same thing as a paid egg in any other roblox game, theres odds and its for an ingame item". They're sold like Roblox's paid eggs: every prize and its odds shown before paying (in the game and in the pass and product descriptions), only in-game items, nothing that pays out Robux or real money, and nobody `PolicyService` restricts is offered them. A coinflip for coins that can be bought is still off the table.
