# Warzone (Draft)
Inspired by the Skybounds Warzone — see [[Skybounds (Minecraft)]].

## Open design questions
1. **Death penalty:** lose everything carried, part of it, or nothing?
2. **Opponents:** players (PvP), monsters (PvE), or both?
3. **Best loot:** materials, blueprints, gear, cosmetics, pets?
4. **Access:** always open, or timed events (e.g., opens every 30 minutes)?

## Constraints
- [Unverified] Combat may raise the maturity rating — check against [[Roblox Policies]].
- Tone (stylized vs realistic) affects rating and audience.
- Must stay opt-in: your island is always safe.
