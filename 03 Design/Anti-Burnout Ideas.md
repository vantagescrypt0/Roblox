# Anti-Burnout Ideas
Ideas to reduce burnout — none are proven for this game yet.

- Offline progress and timers (reason to return, no punishment for leaving).
- Prestige with real unlocks (new mechanics/zones, not just bigger numbers).
- Collection index with set-completion rewards.
- Player trading, with anti-scam UI (two-step confirm, trade log).
- Economy sinks (upgrades, crafting, cosmetics) to slow inflation.
- Controlled power creep per update.
- Update cadence: weekly small event + monthly content drop (example).
- Rotating limited items that return later.
- Server-wide events (Warzone events, bosses, weather).
- Social status: island showcases, leaderboards, titles.
- Seasonal Warzone maps and loot tables.
