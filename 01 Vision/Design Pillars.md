# Design Pillars (Draft)
Every feature should support at least one pillar.

1. **Your island, your story** — owning, building and decorating a place that's yours.
2. **Always growing** — crops, animals and machines keep producing, including offline.
3. **Risk and reward** — the Warzone: fight, loot, escape, bring it home. See [[Warzone]].
4. **Better together** — co-op islands, a busy hub, trading, squads, server events.
5. **Never abandoned** — visible roadmap, steady update rhythm, in-game news.

> Why pillar 5 exists: you stopped playing Islands when updates stopped and the community thinned out.
