# Target Audience

- **Primary:** younger Roblox players (Roblox Kids 5–8 and Roblox Select 9–15 age bands). See [[Roblox Policies]].
- **Platform:** mobile-first UI. [Unverified] A large share of Roblox players are on mobile.

## Monetization line we hold
Monetize with fair value, not pressure on kids:
- ✅ Cosmetics, convenience passes, clear prices.
- ❌ Fake countdown pressure on purchases, hidden or confusing pricing, paid gambling aimed at minors.
See [[Monetization Principles]].
