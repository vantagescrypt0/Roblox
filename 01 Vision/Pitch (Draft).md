# Pitch (Draft)
**Status:** Draft — direction chosen, details still open. See [[Open Questions]].

## One line
Own a floating island you build, farm, craft and automate — then drop into a shared **Warzone** to fight and loot rare materials you bring home to grow even stronger.

## Inspiration
- **Islands (Roblox)** — island ownership, farming, crafting, automation. See [[Islands (Roblox)]].
- **Skybounds (Minecraft server)** — the Warzone where you could loot. Your favorite moment. See [[Skybounds (Minecraft)]].
- We take the *ideas*, not names, maps, UI or assets of either game.

## Why this can work
- [Inference] Islands left a large audience without updates since Nov 2023.
- [Inference] Island = long-term goals; Warzone = short, exciting sessions. Each feeds the other. See [[Core Loop]].
- [Inference] Risk-and-reward looting carries some of the tension of the current "steal" trend, in an opt-in arena. See [[Trends - Oct 2026]].
- The thing that ended Islands for you — no updates, shrinking community — is designed against from day one. See [[Design Pillars]].

## Audience
Younger Roblox players (Kids / Select age bands). See [[Target Audience]].
