**What exists today:** the Combat skill, sword hits, two rough Warzone monsters (Slime, Brute), a Slaying challenge board marked "Coming soon", and a boarded-up Slaying portal in Town. No Slaying World yet.

## The idea
A Slaying World that works like the other Grinding Worlds: its own place, six themed islands, a Combat level to enter each zone. You fight monsters for Combat XP and for drops you sell in Town.

**Mostly safe.** No PvP and no loot lost when you die: you respawn at the zone's start. Monsters hit back for a little damage, enough to matter but not to make it hard. The real risk stays in the Warzone, so younger players can grind Combat without being hunted.

Your Skybounds slayer shots give each zone its own terrain and a giant statue of its monster, so you can see what's ahead from the bridge. The statues here would be our own designs.

| Combat | Monster | Drop |

Earnings would follow the other Grinding Worlds (~2k an hour at the start, up to what the top of those pays; see [[Late-game Rule]]) and Combat would take about 25 hours to 99, fitted with `tools/balance.py`. Monsters stay blocky and cartoonish with no blood, which should help the age rating, though combat could still affect it, so check it against the maturity questionnaire before launch.

## What comes with it
- **A Slayer shopkeeper in Town**, who buys the drops. That's a new NPC. (Beside the portal at first; you moved him to the east lane on 2026-11-08, see the Decision Log.)
- **Combat challenges:** pet, armor and trophy tiers per monster, like the other skills, and the Slaying board opens.
- **Spawners for your island:** slivers from the first 6 monsters craft a spawner of that monster, which you place on your island to fight it there.
