# Trends — October 2026
Researched 2026-10-14. Sources are mostly third-party trackers and blogs; numbers are their estimates.

| Trend | Evidence | Read |
|---|---|---|
| Steal-and-defend | Steal An Egg reported #1 by live players (~1.5M on one tracker), launched 2026-07-25. Steal a Brainrot started the wave in 2025. | [Inference] Hook has lasted 1+ year, but crowded. |
| Idle-grow / cozy | Grow a Garden peaked at 22.3M concurrent (Aug 2025), then fell steeply. | [Inference] Event spikes then fast decay — a burnout warning. |
| Roleplay + sim hybrids | One report: roleplay CCU +~29% YoY, sims −~20%, hybrids "small but growing". | [Unverified] Single source. |
| Other rising | Co-op horror (99 Nights in the Forest), anime fighters, fashion/social. | Less relevant. |

## Reported reasons big hits declined
Event-spike design, bots and exploits, economy trust problems, confusing resets, repetitive updates, competition from the same developer's next game. (Reported explanations, not studies.)
