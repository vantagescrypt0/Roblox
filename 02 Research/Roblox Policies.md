# Roblox Policies to Respect
Check the official Creator Hub before relying on any of this — details change.

## Roblox Kids & Select (2026)
- Roblox Kids = ages 5–8; Roblox Select = ages 9–15 (varies by region).
- Kids accounts can access **Minimal** and **Mild** games; Select can also access **Moderate**.
- Reaching these players requires: age-checked + verified creator account, 2-step verification, a refundable per-game fee or eligible subscription, and passing an evaluation.
- [Unverified] Engagement thresholds (e.g., 500 highly engaged players over 60 days) come from secondary sources.

## Paid random items (eggs, crates bought with Robux)
- Must check `PolicyService:GetPolicyInfoForPlayerAsync` → `ArePaidRandomItemsRestricted` per player (DevForum, May 2026).

## Steal An Egg precedent (Aug 2026)
- Pulled for a few hours; returned without its treadmill video-feed feature. Roblox then added a rule against that feed mechanic.
