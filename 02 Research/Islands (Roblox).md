# Islands (Roblox)

## Facts (with sources)
- Developer: Easy.gg (also makes BedWars).
- Active development ended **2023-11-07**; game stayed open. (Fan wikis.)
- ~2.4B lifetime visits; ~1.2K concurrent, 1.8K peak as of 2026-07-29 (Profitable.app estimate).
- Was renamed from "Skyblock" after a DMCA request. [Unverified] Whether that relates to development ending is unclear.

## From memory / training — [Unverified]
Personal floating island, farming, trees and mining, crafting stations and smelting, automation, animals, fishing (May 2022 update), combat islands with mobs and bosses, shared hub with vendors, trading, co-op, quests, decorative blocks.

## Mechanics (from fan wikis and guide sites)
[Unverified] Not from Easy.gg's own docs. Exact numbers may have changed over updates.

1. **Totems:** passive resource generators bought from a Totem Vendor (Stone 2,000 coins, Iron 4,000, Potato 5,500). Each collects its resource within a 7×7 block range. There was no wood totem; players asked for one for months.
2. **Conveyor factories:** totems → conveyor → smelter → industrial chest. Tier 2 conveyors run at 2× speed (need Workbench Tier 4). Splitters, filters and sensors work as light logic (e.g., a sensor triggers a totem disabler when storage overflows).
3. **Late industrial tier:** Gold Resource Block + Drill gives infinite gold ore. There's a Red Bronze Refinery (electric items), Pumpjacks (oil), Industrial Lumbermill + Sapling Planter (wood, finally automated, but expensive), Flower Pickers and Tree Shakers.
4. **NPC tutorials:** at milestones an NPC visits with a starter pack (e.g., "Jack" gives 1 industrial chest + 8 conveyors after your first steel mill).
5. **Key-gated adventure islands:** kill slimes until one drops the Buffalkor Key (~6.9%), which unlocks the next island. Ores are region-specific; diamond rocks have a 0.25–0.5% chance to spawn enchanted.
6. **Token-summoned bosses:** e.g., the Slime King needs 500 tokens (slimes drop 5/10/25). There are summon cooldowns (25+ min for one boss). Public servers mean others summon bosses too.
7. **Ultra-rare pets:** boss pet eggs drop at ~0.33%, Buffalkor baby at ~0.02%.
8. **Skill-gated trading:** vending machines on your island. Buying needs Economy lvl 25; crafting a machine needs lvl 55. Later added direct trading through a handshake button. [Unverified] Trading was reportedly almost entirely disabled after a duplication exploit (weak source).
9. **Collection quests:** fossil pieces hidden across islands complete a T-Rex skeleton decoration.
10. **Seasonal scarcity:** event items (e.g., Witch Trophy ~6M coins on value lists) became status symbols.
