# Islands (Roblox)

## Facts (with sources)
- Developer: Easy.gg (also makes BedWars).
- Active development ended **2023-11-07**; game stayed open. (Fan wikis.)
- ~2.4B lifetime visits; ~1.2K concurrent, 1.8K peak as of 2026-07-29 (Profitable.app estimate).
- Was renamed from "Skyblock" after a DMCA request. [Unverified] Whether that relates to development ending is unclear.

## From memory / training — [Unverified]
Personal floating island, farming, trees and mining, crafting stations and smelting, automation, animals, fishing (May 2022 update), combat islands with mobs and bosses, shared hub with vendors, trading, co-op, quests, decorative blocks.

## Your experience
- You played it and loved it.
- You stopped when they gave up and the community slowly shrank.

## Lessons
- [Inference] Sandbox depth keeps a loyal core.
- [Inference] Abandonment is the ultimate burnout → [[Design Pillars]] pillar 5.
