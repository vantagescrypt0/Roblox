# Skybounds (Minecraft server)

## Facts (with sources)
- Minecraft Skyblock server; listing says it launched November 2016.
- @PlaySkybounds on X posted about a "global launch" on 2021-07-23.
- The server is now shut down (you confirmed this). So little is documented about it.

## Documented features (from server listings and player posts)
- 100+ challenges plus daily challenges; islands competed on a challenge ladder.
- 10 worlds/warzones with loot and ores, plus dungeons.
- Leveling everywhere: item leveling, player leveling, custom enchantments, skill trees, custom gear sets, quests, AFK-able grinders.
- Co-op: invite unlimited players to your island; visit unlimited islands.
- **Fly tokens:** timed flight items from crates, sellable on the auction house.
- Player economy, seasonal events, competitive leaderboard.
- [Unverified] No details on how any of these were tuned.
