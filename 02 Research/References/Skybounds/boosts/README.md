## How Skybounds did it
- **Boost Gems are permanent unlocks**, "for the entire LIFETIME of SkyBounds!", bought on the web store at $9.99 each.
- **Three trees of five boosts.** Each gem you put in a tree unlocks its next boost, in order ("Current Boosts[0]" to "[5]", then "MAXIMUM BOOST"). 15 gems max out all three.
- **Each season you can reassign your gems** between trees.
- "Boost benefits are subject to change when game balance requires. More Boosts may be added in the future."

| # | Warzone Boosts ("Aimed at helping you in Warzone") | Player Boosts ("Applicable Everywhere") | Island Boosts ("Only works for Island you own") |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Increased damage dealt in PvE (+30%) | Increased XP from mobs (+25%) | Vanilla hopper transfer rate: 2x items |
| 2 | Reduced teleport exit timer (-50%) | Increased economy (+5% on all shop sales) | Ore seed respawn time: 50% faster |
| 3 | Reduced food loss in PvP (-50%) | Extra loot drop (10% chance for extra drop) | Vanilla hopper transfer rate: 4x items |
| 4 | Increased armor durability (+50%) | Increased damage dealt to mobs (+50%) | Ore seed respawn time: 100% faster |
| 5 | Increased max HP (+3 hearts) | Ability to fly (in all non-PvP zones) | Skill XP boost: +25% XP |
