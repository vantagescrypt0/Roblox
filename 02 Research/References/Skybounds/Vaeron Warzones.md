# The Skybounds Warzones, built by Team Vaeron
Source (you sent it, 2026-11-02): https://teamvaeron.com/project/skybounds. "Multi-themed projects intended for wide-spread PvP combat ... several different connected floating islands ... fine-tuned and optimized for functionality during PvP."

This is the bar for our Warzone. These builds are Team Vaeron's work: we follow the idea and the quality, and build our own islands. We don't copy their builds. Their Cartoon map uses famous characters, which we must never use.

## What they all share
1. **A hub in the middle:** one huge round build. On the Robot map it's a citadel with a tall tower, ringed towers, a moat of glowing rings and gates on four sides.
2. **25-30 floating islands** around it, spread out in a net and at different heights, never a flat floor. Sky everywhere between them.
3. **Compass districts:** North, South, East and West each have a big landmark island with its whole name floating over it in chunky 3D letters ("West", "North"), facing the hub, so you always know where you are.
4. **Thin connections:** most links are narrow strings of blocks or bunting-like stepping stones, with a few wide solid bridges between the big islands. You pick routes, get chased, and can get knocked off.
5. **One hero build per island:** a tower, a windmill, a ship, a dinosaur. Every island is recognisable.
6. **Real rock undersides:** tapered stalactites, sometimes with waterfalls off the edge.
7. **Big trees and plants** soften every island, and each map has its own colours.
8. **Cover for fights:** buildings, trees and height differences. Nothing is an open plain.

## Details seen in the photos (looked at closely 2026-11-02)
- **The hub's plaza:** concentric rings, a dark deck, a glowing blue channel crossed by white spokes, a pale rim, small domes at the edge.
- **The stepping stones** are small separate pucks in alternating colours, hanging in a sagging curve, like bunting.
- **The light:** builds are sunlit and bright against a dark navy sky, which is what makes them pop. It isn't a night scene.
- **West district:** a junkyard landmark (giant tyres, a traffic cone, storage tanks) with a palm beach and wreck, a saucer and purple crystal cliffs round it, joined by two wide white bridges and strings of stones.
